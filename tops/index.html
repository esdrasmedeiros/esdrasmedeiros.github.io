TOPs: Topological Mesh Operators

A framework for constructing and editing triangulated 2-manifolds, with and without boundary.

Source archive ↓Documentation ↗Paper PDF ↓

A small set of operations. A complete framework.

TOPs separates operations that change a mesh’s topology from those that modify its combinatorial structure. Together, these primitives support mesh construction and editing within a coherent topological framework.

Thomas Lewiner · Hélio Lopes · Esdras Medeiros · Geovan Tavares · Luiz Velho
Computer Aided Geometric Design · 2010
