Using Physically Based Rendering to Benchmark Structured Light Scanners

Esdras Medeiros · Harish Doraiswamy · Matthew Berger · Claudio T. Silva

Computer Graphics Forum · 33(7), 71–80 · 2014

Paper PDF ↓Supplement PDF ↓GitHub ↗Source ZIP ↓DOI ↗

About the benchmark

Structured light scanning captures detailed 3D geometry, but its accuracy depends on scene materials, lighting, and the projector. This benchmark uses physically based rendering to simulate the acquisition process and compare reconstructed point clouds with ground truth.

The experiments evaluate gray-code patterns, micro-phase shifting, ensemble codes, and unstructured light scanning. They examine errors caused by inter-reflections, sub-surface scattering, and projector defocus, showing how different methods respond to different sources of error.

Evaluation & reproduction

The paper and supplementary material describe the experiments. The repository includes the original source release and instructions for reproducing the benchmark.
